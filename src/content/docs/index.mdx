## How to use these

The videos are spoken in Tamil. These written pages are in English, because a wrong
technical translation is worse than no translation and a real translator has not been paid
yet. Code, commands and error messages are in English either way, which is what you will
paste into a terminal and search for.

Every page carries the versions it was tested against. If yours differ and something
breaks, that is worth a comment at the bottom of the page rather than an hour of doubting
yourself.

Nothing here is behind a login or a paywall, and nothing is going to be.
